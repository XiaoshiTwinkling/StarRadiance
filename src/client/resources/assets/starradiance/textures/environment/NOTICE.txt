Lunar surface imagery and elevation data
========================================

The moon albedo textures in this directory (moon_albedo_*.png) are derived from the
NASA Scientific Visualization Studio "CGI Moon Kit" by Ernie Wright, built from
Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) imagery:

    https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/

The moon normal maps (moon_normal_*.png) are derived from the Lunar Orbiter Laser
Altimeter (LOLA) digital elevation model distributed by the LRO mission:

    https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
    https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/lola.htm

Source: NASA / Goddard Space Flight Center / Scientific Visualization Studio.
